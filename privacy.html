Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your editor: your account, your setup answers, and the projects and media you upload.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • When you use AI features, the material needed for that task is sent to our AI providers (OpenAI and Anthropic) to produce the result.
  • Payments are handled by Whop. We never see or store your card details.
  • Email hello@replotre.com to access, correct or delete your data.

1. Who we are

Replotre ("Replotre", "we", "us") operates the online video editor at replotre.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the website and the Replotre Studio, how we use it and the choices you have. Questions: hello@replotre.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Email and password. Your email address, and your password stored only as a salted one-way hash. We cannot read your password.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google account ID, email address (and whether Google has verified it) and your first name.

Setup answers

What you tell us during setup: the name you'd like us to use, your role, what you make, where and how often you publish, your default frame, caption style, favourite templates, editing experience and team size. We use these to set your studio's defaults and to suggest a plan.

Your content

Projects and timelines, and the media you upload (video, audio and images). To power editing features we also create derived data from your media, such as thumbnails, sampled still frames, waveforms and transcripts, and we store the videos you export.

Billing information

Your plan, its status, billing interval and renewal dates, and your Whop membership reference. Card and payment details are collected and processed by Whop, not by us.

Usage and technical information

Counts we need to apply plan limits (for example, AI requests per day and storage used), and standard server logs such as IP address, time and the request made, which we use for security, abuse prevention and fixing problems.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: sign you in, save and sync your projects, process and export your videos, and run the features you use.
  • To tailor the studio to your setup answers.
  • To manage your plan, apply limits and handle billing through Whop.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to show you important notices about your account in the studio.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or use your content to train AI models.

4. AI features

Some features are powered by third-party AI providers. Only the material needed for the feature you use is sent, and only when you use it:

FeatureProviderWhat is sent
Auto captions, speech-synced titlesOpenAIThe audio track of the clip being transcribed
Visual understanding, Insights, AI DirectorOpenAISampled still frames from your clip, your instructions and a text description of your timeline
Design and refine templates with AIAnthropicYour request and a text description of your timeline

These providers process this data to return results to us under their own API terms and privacy policies. Depth and background effects run on your own device, and that media is not sent to us for that feature. AI output can be wrong, so review it before publishing.

5. Service providers we use

We share information only with providers that help us run Replotre, and only as needed for their role:

  • Railway: hosts our application, database and stored media.
  • Vercel: provides DNS for our domain.
  • Whop: checkout, payments and subscription management.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic: AI features, as described above.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google".

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public, or as part of a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. Google sign-in data

When you sign in with Google we request only the basic openid, email and profile permissions. We use your Google account ID, email address and first name solely to create your Replotre account, sign you in and pre-fill your name. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data, and we do not sell, share or use Google user data for advertising.

Replotre's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove Replotre's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual projects and media in the studio at any time. To delete your account and everything in it, email hello@replotre.com from the address on your account. We will delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax or billing reasons. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to receive a copy of your data. You can download your project files from the studio at any time. To make any other request, email hello@replotre.com. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, hash passwords, keep uploaded media private to your account, use secure sign-in cookies and protect requests against cross-site attacks. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

11. International transfers

Our hosting and service providers may store and process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer data, we rely on our providers' safeguards and contractual commitments.

12. Children

Replotre is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the studio or by email before they take effect, and we will always update the effective date above. Questions or requests: hello@replotre.com.
